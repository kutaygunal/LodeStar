# Lodestar — Support Model

This document describes the support tiers, response targets, and contact
channels available for the Lodestar GNSS/SBAS Integrated Test & Verification
platform.

## 1. Support tiers

Support is included with paid editions of Lodestar. The Evaluation edition does
not include support.

| Tier | Included with | Hours | Response target | Channels |
|------|---------------|-------|-----------------|----------|
| **Community** | Evaluation | Best effort | 5 business days | Email |
| **Standard** | Professional, Team | Business hours (Mon–Fri, 09:00–17:00 local) | 2 business days | Email, web portal |
| **Priority** | Enterprise | 24×7 | 4 hours (critical) | Email, phone, web portal |
| **Premium / On-site** | Enterprise + add-on | 24×7 | 1 hour (critical) | Dedicated engineer, phone, on-site |

## 2. Severity levels

| Severity | Definition | Target response |
|----------|------------|-----------------|
| **S1 — Critical** | System down or data loss; no workaround | 1–4 hours |
| **S2 — High** | Major feature impaired; workaround available | 1 business day |
| **S3 — Medium** | Minor issue; workaround available | 2 business days |
| **S4 — Low** | Cosmetic / documentation / question | 5 business days |

## 3. What is covered

- Installation and configuration assistance.
- Defect diagnosis and resolution.
- Guidance on using the platform's features.
- Access to software updates and patches (paid editions).
- Escalation to engineering for confirmed product defects.

## 4. What is not covered

- Custom development or feature requests (handled under a separate services
  agreement).
- Issues caused by unauthorized modification, misuse, or unsupported
  environments.
- Third-party hardware/software integration beyond documented interfaces.

## 5. Contact channels

- **Support portal:** https://lodestar.example/support
- **Email:** support@lodestar.example
- **Phone (Priority/Premium):** +1-555-0100 (24×7)
- **Licensing:** licensing@lodestar.example

## 6. Reporting a problem

When reporting an issue, please include:

1. Lodestar edition and version.
2. Operating system and build configuration.
3. Steps to reproduce the problem.
4. Any relevant logs or error messages.
5. Expected vs. actual behavior.

## 7. Service-level agreement (SLA)

SLA terms, uptime commitments, and credits are defined in the applicable
license or services agreement. Contact support@lodestar.example for a copy of
the current SLA.
